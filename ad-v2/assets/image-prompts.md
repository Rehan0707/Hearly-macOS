# ImageGen production prompts

All three selected images were created with the built-in ImageGen tool and saved into `assets/`. They are original synthetic advertising imagery. The following prompts were used.

## cafe-human.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium cinematic advertising photograph, landscape 16:9, ideally 2560x1440 or larger. A relatable South Asian female creative professional, around 30, with shoulder-length dark hair, in a soft ivory knit top, sitting on the RIGHT HALF of frame at a wooden table in a lively modern neighborhood cafe, speaking naturally to her open slim silver laptop. Three-quarter side profile, expressive focused face, mid conversation, one hand gesturing naturally. Her laptop is on the center-left, screen facing her so screen not visible to camera. This should feel like an honest important work call, not a posed stock photo. A few distant cafe patrons and barista are softly out of focus in the background. Window light, tactile film grain, gentle amber highlights and cool olive shadows, warm humanity. Left third is darker softly blurred cafe environment with tasteful negative space for large ad typography added separately. Medium-wide composition, laptop and face fully visible. Fine realistic skin texture, beautifully shaped light, shallow depth of field. No text, no watermark, no logos, no headphones, no brand symbols. For a Hearly voice-isolation app motion graphic ad; one unified photographic image, not collage.

## cafe-relief.png

Input/edit target: cafe-human.png.

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit this advertising photo into the warm emotional payoff shot for the same Hearly commercial. Keep exactly the same woman, cafe, ivory knit top, laptop, table and realistic premium cinematographic style. Change her expression and pose: she has finished sharing her idea and is now listening to her laptop with a relaxed genuine smile, shoulders at ease, hands gently resting beside the laptop. A feeling of being understood, quietly confident, not laughing or posed. Slightly tighter camera angle on her face and upper body with the laptop still visible. Preserve the same natural warm window light and softly blurred cafe background. Landscape 16:9. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## colleagues.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. One landscape photographic triptych contact sheet for compositing into an app-ad video call. Three equally wide vertical panels edge-to-edge with no borders, no text, no UI. Each panel is a different natural webcam-like head-and-shoulders portrait of a friendly professional looking toward the camera and listening with a slight genuine smile. LEFT: Black male creative director around 35, short hair, muted blue crewneck, soft daylight home studio. CENTER: East Asian woman around 30, straight shoulder-length hair, warm beige blouse, quiet light minimalist home office. RIGHT: white man around 45 with short beard and glasses, deep olive shirt, softly lit bookcase background. Each person's entire head comfortably inside their own panel. Real skin texture, warm truthful human expressions, imperfect natural backgrounds, cinematic grade but believable video call camera angle. No stock-photo grins, no headphones, no hand gestures, no brand marks, no watermarks. Three separate people, each centered in a third of the image. Overall 3:1 aspect ratio, high resolution.
